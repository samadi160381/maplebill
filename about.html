Our Story

MapleBill was born out of frustration. Like thousands of Canadian freelancers, our founders were spending hours every month wrestling with spreadsheets, manually calculating HST, and chasing clients for payments.

Generic invoicing tools weren't built with Canada in mind — they didn't understand provincial tax differences, didn't support bilingual invoices, and charged per invoice. We decided to build something better.

What We Believe

  • Simplicity wins. Invoicing should take minutes, not hours.
  • Canada-first. HST, GST, PST, QST — we handle all of it automatically.
  • Fair pricing. No per-invoice fees. No surprise charges. One flat rate.
  • Your data is yours. We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

By the Numbers

  • 12,000+ Canadian freelancers trust MapleBill
  • $48M+ invoiced through our platform
  • All 13 provinces and territories supported
  • EN/FR bilingual invoice support
